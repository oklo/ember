# Mass and composition survey

The survey should locate the hydrogen-burning minimum mass and measure
lifetimes across initial mass, hydrogen, helium and metal abundance pattern.
Each track starts from Hayashi contraction with source tables covering its
anticipated thermal and composition range.

Record central hydrogen exhaustion separately from extinction of sustained
shell burning. Near the stellar–brown-dwarf boundary, demonstrate sustained
hydrogen burning rather than assigning a main-sequence lifetime to a cooling
object. Test endpoint ages with spatial and time refinement and physical
alternatives; restart reproducibility alone does not establish accuracy.

A lifetime curve labelled by mass and metallicity also assumes a helium-enrichment
relation and metal pattern. Vary those assumptions. Greater opacity competes
with diminished hydrogen fuel and composition-driven luminosity changes;
a lifetime maximum or its location must emerge from the calculations.

The first target is the fully supported 0.1-solar-mass track. Broader coverage
requires appropriate nuclear physics and tables, not just a changed mass parameter.
