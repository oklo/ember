COMMON QUALIFICATIONS
All plotted trajectories use reviewed accepted numerical states. The latest Z=.005 cooling segment is a separately hash-certified accepted prefix from an invocation terminated by its CPU limit (SIGXCPU); later rejected trials reached the hydrogen-opacity coverage edge. The certificate verifies saved states, input identities and numerical gates; it does not convert the interrupted run into a clean completion. Other failed continuations are excluded. The opacity-extrapolation segment is separately identified; it ended cleanly at its CPU budget, with subsequent rejected trials showing an unresolved mixing transient. Its saved rise is not a peak.

The delayed branches use maximum steps of 5 and 2 Gyr after the same 130.03 Gyr checkpoint. Earlier evolution, spatial mesh and base microphysics are shared. Delayed warming occurs in both, but timing and amplitude are not quantitatively converged. The extra opacity scenario uses a bounded log-opacity secant in elemental hydrogen abundance (anchors .70/.75, limit .80), with no extension in temperature, density or metallicity. Native acceptance and energy-audit tolerances are unchanged. This is an explicit approximation, not a validated uncertainty bound.

First saved stable interface means radiative_boundaries > 0. Convective mass fraction of unity can include disconnected convective regions.
